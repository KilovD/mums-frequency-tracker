    Keep your record safe

    Backup & help

    Your diary belongs to you. No account or sign-in needed.

    Your entries stay on this device

    Records are saved in this browser. They are not sent to a server or shared between your phone and computer.

    Clearing browser data or using private browsing can lose your diary. Download a backup regularly and before changing devices or web addresses.

    Add to your home screen

    Install the app for easy access. Open it online once to prepare it for offline use.

    1. iPhone / iPad: open the hosted app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    2. Android: open it in Chrome and choose Install app or Add to Home screen from the menu.
    3. Computer: use the install icon in Chrome or Edge’s address bar when available.

    Making an entry

    Tap one of the two large buttons. The time is captured when you tap, so you can take your time entering the amount.

    For a drink, choose the drink type and enter the amount in millilitres (mL). For urination, enter the amount measured. The shortcut amounts are optional; you can type any whole amount.

    Use Edit beside an entry to change or delete it. Dates and times stay as recorded, even if you travel.

    Sharing a chart

    Open Frequency chart and choose your dates. Export CSV creates a spreadsheet-friendly diary. Print / save PDF opens your device’s print options.

    This is a record of what you enter, with no fluid targets or medical interpretation. Bring the chart to an appointment if it is helpful.
